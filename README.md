Entry for [The Funniest Honest Appliance Error](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-funniest-honest-appliance-error-adec4450) on make.fun.

> Error: keep-warm 14 hours. Your rice is now a brick. I kept it warm. That was the deal.

The joke is a rice cooker reporting, completely flatly, that it kept the rice warm for 14 hours until it turned into a brick. It did its job perfectly — and that is exactly the disaster. No apology, no malfunction, just a machine proudly fulfilling the deal while dinner becomes construction material. Dry wit, delivered as an error message.
